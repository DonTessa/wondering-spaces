Before you email

Please keep your first email brief. Your name, the best way to reach you, and a line about what you're looking for is plenty. Please don't include personal details about your health, history or circumstances at this stage. We can talk about those safely when we first speak.

If you need help now

I can't respond to emergencies. If you need urgent support:

  • In an emergency, call 999 or go to A&E
  • NHS 24: call 111, any time
  • Samaritans: call 116 123, any time
  • Breathing Space: call 0800 83 85 87
