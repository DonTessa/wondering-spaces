Paint nights

Wondering Spaces Paint Nights

Relaxed sip and paint evenings, where you're guided step by step to create your own painting. No art experience is necessary and you don't need to be good at art.

Painting with a purpose

The profits from every paint night help fund free and low-cost individual art psychotherapy sessions with Don, an HCPC-registered art psychotherapist. These sessions are for people in the "missing middle": those who experience difficulties that some consider too complex for primary care, but that do not meet the threshold for secondary mental health services.

So while you enjoy an evening of painting, you're also helping someone access therapy they might not otherwise be able to afford.

What to expect

The evening
A guided painting session, with time to chat, relax and take your painting home at the end
Materials
Canvas, paints, brushes and aprons are all provided
Drinks
Bring your own drinks where the venue allows. Details are given for each event.
Venues
Venues vary, in Glasgow and the surrounding areas
Dates
New dates are shared here and on the Wondering Spaces Facebook and Instagram pages

A social evening, not therapy

Paint nights are social art evenings. They are not art psychotherapy sessions.

To protect the therapeutic relationship, current or recent therapy clients of Wondering Spaces aren't able to attend. Thank you for understanding.
