About · Inside-Outsider

Inside-Outsider exhibition

The values behind Inside-Outsider are the values behind Wondering Spaces: that lived experience is a valuable form of knowledge, and that no one should have to hide parts of themselves to be understood.

In October 2023, I conceived and led Inside-Outsider, an anti-stigma exhibition of 53 artworks by regulated mental health professionals and trainees with lived experience of mental health difficulties. It ran at the Out of the Blue Drill Hall in Edinburgh, alongside a virtual gallery, as part of the Scottish Mental Health Arts Festival.

As arts coordinator and project lead, I curated the exhibition and organised its fully booked opening night. The project was supported by BAAT Scotland, the Thrive Arts small grants fund and in2gr8mentalhealth CIC.
